# 📊 Business & Risk Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on analyzing business performance, cash flow, and loan risk to support data-driven decision-making.

The goal is to answer:
- Where is the revenue coming from?
- How efficient is the cash collection?
- Where are the financial risks?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total Sales: **755M**
- Total Collected: **581M**
- Cash represents ~**40%** of total sales

### 🏢 Branch Performance
- **West Hub** is the top-performing branch in terms of sales

### 💰 Cash Analysis
- Highest contribution from:
  - Mobile App
  - In-Store
- Website is underperforming
- Drop in Q2, recovery in Q3 & Q4

### 🏦 Loan & Risk Analysis
- Total Loans: **10.2K**
- Outstanding (+90 days): **4.68%**
- Majority are **Low Risk (~85%)**
- Highest outstanding in:
  - Cairo
  - Alexandria
